# Content Rating — TriciGo Conductor

> Answers ready to paste/select in **Google Play Console → Policy → App content → Content ratings (IARC)** and **App Store Connect → App → Age Rating**.
>
> TriciGo Conductor is the driver-side ride-hailing/work utility app. **No objectionable content.** Interactive elements: (a) driver↔rider **in-app chat during an active trip**, (b) **live location sharing** with the assigned rider during a trip (foreground + background while online on a trip). Result: **Everyone / 4+** with "Users Interact" + "Shares Location" descriptors. Background location is declared separately (see `background-location-declaration-form.md`) and does **not** affect the content/age rating.

---

## 1. Google Play — IARC questionnaire

**Category:** App (not a game) → "Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other".

**Email for IARC certificate:** soporte@tricigo.com

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Violence (cartoon, fantasy, or realistic)? | **No** |
| Sexual or suggestive content / nudity? | **No** |
| Profanity or crude humor? | **No** |
| Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco)? | **No** |
| Gambling (simulated or real-money)? | **No** |
| Frightening/disturbing content for young users? | **No** |
| User-generated content / can users interact or exchange messages? | **Yes** — the driver and the assigned rider exchange short text messages **during an active trip only**. No public feed or social browsing. |
| Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users? | **Yes** — while online on an active trip, the driver's live location is shared with the assigned rider (incl. background, for trip tracking). Not shared outside an active trip. |
| Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? | **No.** The driver does **not** pay in-app; they **receive earnings** into an internal wallet (CUP). No digital-goods purchases. |
| Ads? | **No** |
| News app? | **No** — work/utility app for drivers. |

**Expected rating:** ESRB **Everyone** · PEGI **3** · USK **0** · with **"Users Interact"** and **"Shares Location"** labels.
